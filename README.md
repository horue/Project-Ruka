##
**About**

Project Ruka is a research initiative focused on developing a low-cost bionic hand using accessible hardware and modern intelligence techniques.
The goal is to create an affordable, functional prosthetic solution by combining:

* Off-the-shelf microcontrollers
* EMG (electromyography) sensors
* Machine learning and AI models for signal interpretation and motor control

## 
**First Prototype**

Our first prototype achieved a cost well below 1% of the price of a commercial prosthesis of the same type, proving the feasibility of an extremely affordable alternative.

##
**Goal**

Ruka aims to make advanced prosthetic technology more accessible while providing a flexible platform for experimentation, innovation, and future improvements.
